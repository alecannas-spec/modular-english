Delete your Modular English account

Elimina il tuo account Modular English

Delete it in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Modular English.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account, then confirm twice.

Your profile (name, e-mail, level), progress, cart and settings are erased from the phone at once. Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

Nell'app: Profilo → “Delete my account” → conferma. I dati vengono cancellati subito.

No longer have the app? Ask us

Write from the e-mail address you used, with the subject Delete my account. We reply and delete any data we hold about you (e.g. support e-mails) within 30 days.

support@gilo-international.com

Scrivi a support@gilo-international.com con oggetto “Delete my account”. Cancelliamo entro 30 giorni.

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted: profile, learning progress, cart, settings, and any support e-mails on request.
  • Not held by us: the app keeps your data only on your phone — there is no user database on our side.
  • Kept by Google: your purchase history stays with your Google account (Google's records, needed for tax and refunds). Bought courses can be restored after reinstalling.
  • Memberships are not cancelled by deleting the account. Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
