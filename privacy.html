Privacy Policy — Modular English

September 2026 · Version 2.0

1. Who We Are

Modular English (OK Modular English) is published by Alessandro Cannas, a private individual based in Sardegna, Italy, who is the Data Controller.

Contact: support@gilo-international.com

2. What Data We Collect

• Profile: the name, e-mail address and English level you enter. Your password is checked on the phone and never stored or sent anywhere.

• Learning data: courses bought, dialogues completed, progress, cart.

• All of the above is stored ONLY on your phone. We run no user database; we cannot see it.

• Purchases: handled by Google Play. We receive the product bought and a purchase token so the app can confirm the purchase is genuine. We never see your card or payment details.

3. How We Use Your Data

• To show your profile and keep your progress on your phone.
• To unlock the courses and memberships you have paid for.
• To answer you when you write to support.

We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we show no advertising.

4. Microphone & Speech Recognition

The Repeat button uses your microphone so you can practise pronunciation. Your speech is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service (on Android, normally Google's), which may process the audio on the phone or on Google's servers under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

The app receives only the text, compares it with the sentence on the phone, and shows the result. No audio is recorded, stored or sent to us. You can revoke microphone permission at any time in your phone's Settings.

5. Third-Party Services

• Google Play — app distribution, payments, subscriptions.
• Google speech recognition and text-to-speech — see section 4.
• Cloudflare — hosts the small service that confirms a purchase with Google. It receives the product ID and purchase token only, and keeps no logs of personal data.

6. Data Retention & Deleting Your Account

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it.

Delete it at any time in Profile → Delete my account. This erases your profile, progress, cart and settings immediately. Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

Courses you bought remain linked to your Google account (restore them with Restore Purchases). A membership is not cancelled by deleting the account — cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

If you have written to support, ask us at support@gilo-international.com to delete those e-mails.

7. Children

Modular English is intended for users aged 13 and over. Younger learners should use it with a parent or teacher. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has contacted us, write to support@gilo-international.com and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR (EU 2016/679) and Italian law (D.Lgs. 196/2003 as amended) you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to data portability. As your data is on your phone, most of this is in your hands directly; for anything else write to support@gilo-international.com — we reply within 30 days.

9. Cookies & Tracking

No cookies, no advertising ID, no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown here with a new date, and material changes will be announced in the app.
