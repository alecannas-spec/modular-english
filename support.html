Restoring a purchase

Already bought a situation or a membership and it's not showing up (new phone, reinstalled app)? Open the app, go to Profile → Restore Purchases, and sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Managing or cancelling a membership

Memberships (Gold, Silver, Bronze) are billed and managed through Apple, not through the app. To change plan, cancel, or check your renewal date: on your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, then select Modular English.

Deleting your account

You can delete your profile, progress and settings at any time from Profile → Delete my account inside the app. This does not automatically cancel an active membership — cancel that separately in Settings → Subscriptions, as above.

Something not working?

Tell us what happened, which screen you were on, and your phone model if you can — it helps us fix it faster. Email support@gilo-international.com.
